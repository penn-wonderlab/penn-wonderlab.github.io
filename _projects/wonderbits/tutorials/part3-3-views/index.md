Filters let you look at the space from a particular angle. When an angle turns out to be worth returning to, save it as a View — the whole combination, not a snapshot of what happened to match that day.

## In this clip

- Building a perspective: questions and claims tagged #collaboration
- **Save View** keeps the whole filter combination, shown back to you before you commit
- Naming it, and choosing whether it's private to you or shared with everyone
