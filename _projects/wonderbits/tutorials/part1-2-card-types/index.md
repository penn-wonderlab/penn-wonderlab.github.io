Adding a card starts by asking what kind of idea you're adding. That question is doing real work: the types are a small shared vocabulary for the moves that build knowledge.

## In this clip

- **Bit** — a raw thought you can't categorize yet
- **Question** — something you want to open up
- **Claim** — a statement you want to test
- **Evidence** — what you test a claim against
- **Synthesis** — several ideas pulled together
- **Draw** — a sketch
- Changing a card's type by clicking its symbol

> **Try it:** Look at a card you wrote earlier. Is it still the type you gave it? If your thinking moved, change it.
