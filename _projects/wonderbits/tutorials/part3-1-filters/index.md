A class space that's working holds more than anyone can read at once. Filters are how you cut it down to the part you need right now, and they stack.

## In this clip

- Clicking a card type narrows the canvas to just those cards
- The bar above the canvas names the active filter and counts what's in view
- Types **add**: click Question, then Claim, and you get both
- Narrowing further by tag, time, author, or reaction
- Stacking filters — questions tagged #collaboration, or claims from this week

> **Try it:** Try asking your space a question you actually have: which questions has nobody built on yet? Filter to Question, then narrow by time.
