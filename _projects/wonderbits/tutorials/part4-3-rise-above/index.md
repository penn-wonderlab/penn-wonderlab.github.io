After a long stretch of work a space holds a great many ideas, and the clutter starts making it hard to add anything new. Rise Above distils what the class worked out into a new starting floor, without losing what came before.

## In this clip

- The level bar, which appears once you pull back far enough
- Rise Above shows you **which ideas would rise, before anything happens**
- The rule it used — connections, idea type, recency, and uptake, with room kept for quieter ideas
- Stepping down a level to find everything still there, and back up again
- Resetting a level: only the top layer dissolves, and no ideas are ever deleted
- Rising and resetting are teacher moves — available to the owner and space managers, not every member

> **Try it:** Look at your space and ask what the class has actually settled. If you can name three or four ideas everything else now builds on, that's a level waiting to be made.
