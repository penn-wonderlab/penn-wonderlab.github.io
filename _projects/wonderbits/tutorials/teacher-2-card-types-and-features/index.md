A space arrives with sensible defaults, and most of them are worth keeping. These are the two settings worth revisiting: the words your class thinks in, and what the canvas asks them to notice.

## In this clip

- The built-in types — Question, Claim, Evidence and the rest — and switching any of them off
- Creating a type your subject actually uses: a name, an icon, a colour, and a line on when to reach for it
- Visibility — shared with the space, or personal, which everyone else simply sees as **Bit**
- Removing a type later leaves the cards using it alone; it only disappears from the picker
- **Canvas Features**: Pulse, Landmarks, Quick add, and Voice Mode with the privacy level you set
- Switching one off, because a canvas with everything on asks students to attend to too much at once

> **Try it:** Read back what your class wrote last week and ask which card type they kept reaching for and didn't have. That's usually the one worth adding.
