## In this clip

- Joining a class with a code, or opening a space you're already in
- Panning and zooming around the canvas
- What a card is, and how the **+** button adds one
- Picking a type, writing, and moving a card
- How your changes reach the rest of the class in real time

> **Try it:** Open your own space and add one card for something you're curious about. Don't worry about getting it right — a card can be edited or retyped any time.
