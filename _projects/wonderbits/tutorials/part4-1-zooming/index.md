Zooming in Wonderbits does more than change how big things look. Each level shows you a different amount of each card, and at the furthest level a handful of ideas hold on to their text while the rest shrink to marks.

## In this clip

- **Engage** — full text, and the only level where you can edit an idea
- **Scan** — cards shorten to their opening line, enough to find your way around
- **Map** — the space becomes a shape you can navigate
- The percentage readout, and clicking the minimap to jump the view
- Landmarks: the ideas that stay legible at map level, chosen by score
- Why a particular card is a landmark, and how a teacher can spotlight one the score missed

> **Try it:** Zoom your own space right out and see which ideas hold their text. Do they match what you'd have picked as the landmarks yourself?
