Sooner or later a space has several ideas circling the same point. A synthesis isn't another idea on the pile — it's your account of what the others add up to.

## In this clip

- Selecting the ideas that belong together
- Choosing **Synthesize** to open a blank synthesis above them
- Writing what the ideas mean together — this is yours to write, not a generated summary
- How the saved synthesis connects itself to the ideas it covers
- Folding the ideas underneath away with the stack button, and unfolding them again

> **Try it:** Scan your space for two or three cards making the same point in different words. That's a synthesis waiting to be written.
