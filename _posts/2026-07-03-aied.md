Wonder Lab members presented two projects at the [27th International Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Education (AIED 2026)](https://festival-of-learning-2026.info/), held as part of the Festival of Learning in Seoul, Republic of Korea.

- [**InkSpire: An LLM-Powered System for Designing and Generating Disciplinarily Aligned and Context-Aware Reading Scaffolds**](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-4231/itb26_s3s2.pdf), presented at the Seventh International Workshop on Intelligent Textbooks. Authors: Yuwei Liang, Ruiqian Wang, and Bodong Chen.
- [**CraftPad: Sustaining Teacher Professional Judgment in Human-AI Collaborative Lesson Design**](https://open-aied.github.io/HAI-Agency/), presented at the HAI-Agency Workshop on Orchestrating Human and AI Agency for Proactive and Reflective Learning. Authors: Jiayu Cheng, Vivian Leung, Shiyang Zhang, and Bodong Chen.

Congratulations to the teams on sharing their work with the international AIED community!
