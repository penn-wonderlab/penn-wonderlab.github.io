How's it going?

Hit reply and tell us — what's working, what's awkward, what your students say when they open it. Short answers are welcome.

Telling us something is broken, or confusing

In the app, click your name in the top-right corner, then Send feedback. Thirty seconds: pick a category, say what happened, say how much it got in your way.

Two things worth knowing:

  • Your students see the same button. If something confuses a class, we want to hear it from the person who was confused.
  • It sends the page you were on and basic browser details. It does not send your canvas content — so please don't paste student work or names into the box.

Class Insights

If you own or co-manage a space, the small bar-chart icon in the top toolbar opens Class Insights in a new tab, so you can keep your space open beside it.

It answers what's hard to eyeball on a canvas: who has been active this week, whose cards nobody has read, who is building on whom, whether ideas are being revised or only added. Set the period anywhere from 24 hours to all time.

On privacy: it shows how much students used the AI helpers and what topics came up across the class. It never shows what a student wrote to them.

When the canvas gets busy

A space that is working outgrows one screen. Three moves, easiest first:

  • Filters — narrow to what you need by type, tag, time, author, or what you haven't read yet. They stack.
  • Views — once a set of filters is useful, save it and reopen it next lesson instead of rebuilding it.
  • Inspect — read the same cards as an outline, a timeline, or a table. Good for marking, and it exports.

When the ideas need organizing rather than the view: Groups to gather related cards, Synthesize to pull several into one higher-order card, and Pages to give a unit or a group its own canvas.

Two you may not have turned on

In Space Settings → Canvas Features: Landmarks keeps your class's key ideas readable when you zoom out, and Quick add gives you a bar for typing many ideas in fast — useful for a whole-class brainstorm. Both are per-space, and both are off until you switch them on. Card types and features →
